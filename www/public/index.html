heron-polyfeed-reader

A small local feed reader and site monitor: polls RSS, Atom and RDF feeds and watches sites for downtime, content change and TLS certificate expiry. Zig, vendored SQLite with FTS5, localhost-only web UI.

This site is served from the www/public/ category of the repository's site-operations bundle. Operational material (DNS, TLS, headers, profiles, tests, runbooks) is deliberately not part of the published surface.
